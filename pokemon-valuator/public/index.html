How it works

Three steps from phone camera to a dollar figure.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Take a clear, well-lit photo of the face of the card — name and collector number visible.

  2. 02

    Identify

    OCR pulls the Pokémon name, HP, and set number, then ranks catalogue matches.

  3. 03

    Price

    Live market, low, mid, and high quotes from TCGPlayer (USD) and Cardmarket (EUR).
