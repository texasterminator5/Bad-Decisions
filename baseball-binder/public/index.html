How it works

Point your phone at a standard 9-pocket page. Keep the sheet flat, fill the frame, and shoot in decent light so names and card numbers stay sharp.

  1. We carve the photo into nine slots (binder grid).
  2. OCR reads player, year, set, and number on each crop.
  3. Matches hit the MyCardfolio catalogue; guide prices come from SportsCardsPro (raw low / mid / gem high).

Values are marketplace guides, not formal appraisals. Condition, grading, and parallels move the number a lot — tap eBay sold comps for live sales.
